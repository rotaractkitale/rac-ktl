About the Club

Who we are as Rotaract Club of Kitale.

A club of young adults who are dedicated to finding innovative solutions to the world's challenges while developing leadership skills, making friends, and having fun through service. In respective of the seven areas of focus embraced by Rotary international, we are committed to make impact in these areas;

  • 1. Peacebuilding
  • 2. Disease prevention and treatment
  • 3. Water, sanitation, and hygiene
  • 4. Maternal and child health
  • 5. Basic education and literacy
  • 6. Economic and community development
  • 7. Supporting and protecting the environment

    • 🤝

    Peacebuilding

    We work towards creating a peaceful community by promoting dialogue,understanding and conlict resolution.

    🌍

    Disease Prevention and Treatment

    We have led efforts in both large and small scale initiatives; Our main achievement is the compliance of rotary top priority project o polio eradication that has helped to reduce global polio cases by 99.9% since 1988, and we continue to support vaccination campaigns and health education to prevent diseases.

    🚀

    Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene

    We have implemented various initiatives in line with the WASH initiative to improve access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene initiative.Our efforts include building water facilities in conjuction with our parent club Rotary,The rotary clubs of Goochland, Fluvana, Innsbrook,Richmond,Brandermill, Virginia alongside Shekinah glory to provide clean water to the Motosiet community.

    💡

    Maternal and Child Health

    We have participated in health camapings such as blood donations drives and maternal health awareness programs along with other health initiatives.

    📚

    Basic Education and Literacy

    We have made an effort o taking up schools and making 'our own schools'by providing basic education alongside hygiene and sanitation to the children in our local community. .

    🌱

    Economic and Community Development

    We have implemented projects that promote economic growth, job creation, and community development in our local area.

    🌳
