Who is Rotary?

Rotary is a global network of neighbors,friends, leaders and problem solvers who work together to take action to create lasting change in communities around the world. With over 1.2 million members around the world.

Our mission

We provide service to others,promote integrity and advance world understanding, goodwill and peace through our fellowship of business, professional and community leaders.

Our vision

Together we see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change across the globe in our communities and in ourselves.

Rotaract is part of the Rotary family.

Rotaract gives young adults an opportunity to serve communities, develop leadership, build friendships, grow professionally, and connect with a global movement of service.

Rotary clubs and Rotaract clubs work together through service projects, mentorship, leadership development, community partnerships, and international collaboration.

Why it matters

Rotary gives Rotaract members a bigger platform for service.

Through Rotary, Rotaractors can connect with mentors, partner clubs, district events, global service opportunities, and a wider community of people committed to doing good.
