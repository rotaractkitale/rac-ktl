How to Join

Become a member of Rotaract Club of Kitale.

Rotaract is open to young adults who want to serve, learn, lead, make friends, and grow personally and professionally.

Your membership journey

You do not need to know everything about Rotaract before joining. Start by attending a fellowship, meeting members, joining an activity, and learning how the club works.


Contact Us to Join
1. Attend a club fellowship
2. Meet members and ask questions
3. Participate in one service activity
4. Fill the membership interest form
5. Get inducted and serve actively
