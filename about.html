About the Club

The Rotaract club of Kitale is a youth led service organization operating under the rotary foundation in Kitale ,Trans Nzoia county. The club was chartered in 2018 and has brought together young professionals who are committed to service,leadership and positive social change. Since its establishment, the club has focused on empowering young people to become responsible leaders while addressing community needs through volunteer service projects. Members participate in initiatives aimed at improving healthcare, education, environmental conservation, youth empowerment and economic development.

Key Achievements

  • Community Service. THe club has actively participated in projects that improves wellness of vulnerable groups including schools, support programmes, mentorship activities, community clean up exercises and health awareness campaigns .
  • Safe Water And Sanitation campaigns. One of our club's notable achievement was its participation alongside the Rotary club of Kitale in implementing the Motosiet safe water project. The project improved access to clean water for approximately 2000 residents through the construction of a borehole. solar powered pump system, water storage facilities and sanitation infrastructure.
  • Youth Empowerment. The club has implemented various programs to empower young people, including leadership training, skill development workshops, and mentorship opportunities. With our professional development initiatives, we have helped young people acquire the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in their careers and make a positive impact in their communities.
  • Blood Donation. The club has organized several blood donation drives to support the local community and ensure a sufficient supply of blood for medical emergencies.
  • Environmental Conservation. The club has been actively involved in environmental conservation efforts, including tree planting campaigns, waste management initiatives, and awareness programs to promote sustainable practices in the community.
